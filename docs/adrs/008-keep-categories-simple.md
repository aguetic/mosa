Keep categories simple.

The types:

- Entity
  - Item: Physical things or documented museum holdings that the research concerns.
  - Agent: People or groups capable of acting, creating records or making assertions.
  - Place: Spatial locations
  - Source: Things from which information is obtained.
- Claim: Assertions extracted from or supported by sources. connects an entity to a value and records the supporting source
- Event (for phase 2)
